# Problem Statement

## 1. Problem Overview

Many students and job seekers understand English grammar but struggle to speak confidently in interviews, presentations, and daily conversations.

Their biggest challenge is not learning English—it is practicing spoken communication consistently while receiving useful feedback.

---

## 2. Current Solutions

Existing platforms include:

- Duolingo
- ELSA Speak
- YouTube
- Online English courses
- Offline spoken English institutes

---

## 3. Problems with Existing Solutions

### Duolingo

Focuses more on vocabulary and grammar than real speaking confidence.

### YouTube

Provides passive learning.

Users watch videos instead of practicing.

### Offline Classes

Expensive.

Limited feedback.

Fixed schedule.

### AI Chatbots

Can answer questions but usually do not evaluate complete speaking performance over time.

---

## 4. Proposed Solution

SpeakForge AI provides daily speaking challenges.

Users record their responses.

Artificial Intelligence evaluates:

- Pronunciation
- Fluency
- Vocabulary
- Grammar
- Confidence

The application stores progress and provides personalized improvement suggestions.

---

## 5. Success Criteria

The product is successful if users:

- Practice speaking consistently.
- Improve speaking confidence.
- Reduce hesitation.
- Improve pronunciation.
- Track measurable progress over time.
